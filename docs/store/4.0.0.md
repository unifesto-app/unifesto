## Paid online sessions (guideline 3.1.1)

The app sells **online live sessions** through Razorpay (`useSessionRegistration`). Apple treats one-to-many live digital services consumed in the app as requiring In-App Purchase. Only one-to-one services (3.1.3(d)) and real-world goods and services (3.1.3(e)) are exempt. In-person event tickets are fine.

**Decided: hide paid checkout on iOS.** Paid sessions on iOS show their details with the note "Registration for paid sessions isn't available in the iOS app." There's no link out (anti-steering). Free sessions, and joining a session bought on the web or Android, still work. Enforced in `useSessionRegistration` (`isPaidSessionBlocked`) and on the session screen. Can be reversed by OTA if IAP is added later.
