product

## Users

Unifesto **attendees** — college students in India using the mobile app to live their
campus event life. Context: on their phone (iOS/Android), on the move — between classes,
at the venue, in the hostel. This is the consumer-facing product; design serves the
in-the-moment task, fast and one-handed.

Jobs to be done:
- **Discover & register.** Browse events and spaces, register in seconds.
- **Attend.** Hold tickets and QR codes for check-in; get into events without friction.
- **Transact.** Pay via Razorpay, manage a wallet.
- **Belong & grow.** Refer friends (referrals), chat, manage profile and support — the
  social and account layer around events.

## Product Purpose

The Unifesto mobile app is the student's home for campus events — the pocket product where
discovery, registration, ticketing, payments, and community come together. It replaces the
scattered mix of forms, screenshots, and group chats with one fast, native app: find an
event, register, pay, get your QR ticket, walk in.

It exists to make attending campus events effortless and a little exciting. Success looks
like a student going from "what's happening this week?" to a scannable ticket in their
wallet in under a minute — and coming back because it's the easiest way to not miss out.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, dark, AI-premium — and unmistakably student-native. This is the black-canvas,
electric-blue Unifesto identity in its purest form: fast, high-contrast, and modern, the
kind of app that looks like a serious tech product but feels made for campus. Voice is
direct, energetic, and a little proud. Native-feeling on both platforms (iOS glass/SF
symbols, Android system UI), never a stretched website.

Emotional goals: **excitement** (something worth showing up for), **confidence** (this
just works), and **belonging** (made for our campus).

## Anti-references

- **Not a generic college portal.** No institutional student-information-system energy,
  no dated list screens.
- **Not cluttered / Eventbrite-y.** Discovery should feel curated and quick, not an
  ad-heavy, dense listing marketplace.
- **Not corporate SaaS.** No enterprise-app coldness. Premium but with student energy;
  a consumer app, not a B2B tool.

## Design Principles

- **Mobile-first, one-handed, fast.** Every core task (find, register, pay, show ticket)
  is reachable with a thumb and completes quickly. Momentum is the feeling.
- **Native, not ported.** Respect each platform's conventions (safe areas, gestures, glass
  effects, system UI) so it feels like a real iOS/Android app, not a web view.
- **High-contrast on the black canvas.** Decisive contrast and the electric-blue accent
  carry hierarchy on the dark surface; nothing dead-weight, nothing muddy.
- **The ticket is sacred.** Tickets, QR, and payment flows are the highest-stakes moments —
  they must be unmistakably clear, reliable, and reassuring, even offline or on a bad connection.
- **Delight without noise.** Motion and polish add excitement, but never at the cost of
  speed, clarity, or reliability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** equivalents for mobile. On the pure-black (#000000) canvas,
  verify text/background contrast: body ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. Watch the muted slate
  grays (`#94a3b8`, `#64748b`) for small secondary text — lighten where they fall short.
- **Respect reduced motion.** Provide reduced-motion alternatives for gradients, glass
  blur, and animated transitions; never gate content behind an animation.
- **Don't rely on the blue accent alone** to convey state (success/warning/error already
  have distinct colors — pair them with icons/labels too) so color-blind students aren't lost.
- **Touch-friendly & resilient.** Adequate tap targets, readable sizes without zoom, and
  layouts and flows that hold up on lower-end Android devices and slow/unstable connections
  common in the Indian market. Ticket/QR display must survive poor connectivity.
