# Unifesto 5.0.0 store listing

The one source for the App Store and Google Play listing text and media of 5.0.0. Paste from here; if a store field changes, change it here first. Rules: no em dashes, no caps headers, only features live in 5.0.0, "My" for the user's own things, spaces never circles.

`node count.js` checks every field against its store limit.

## Both stores

**Name / title (30):** Unifesto: Events & Ticketing

**Description (4000):**

```
Unifesto is where campus and community events happen. Find what's on, book in seconds, and walk in with your ticket ready. Running the event? Do all of it from the same app.

Find your next event
College fests, hackathons, workshops, concerts, cultural nights and meetups. Browse by city and category, see what's on this week, and save the ones you like.

Book in seconds
Register for free events in one tap, or pay securely for tickets. Book once for your whole group or team and invite them by email. Answer the organiser's questions right in the app.

Your ticket, ready at the door
Every ticket keeps its QR code, even offline. Up next sits at the top of My tickets, on your Home Screen widget and in your calendar.

Be part of it live
Vote in polls, ask and upvote questions, and play quizzes from your ticket while the event is on. When it ends, find results and certificates on your past tickets.

Hackathons, sorted
Sprint is your team hub: rooms, problem statements, judging, results and a help desk in one place. Team leads can add members, replace a teammate or hand over the lead.

Help when you need it
Ask for help from Profile and follow every request in My help. The bell on Home keeps every update, and quiet hours hold notifications overnight.

For organisers: the Space tab
Everything you need to run an event, on your phone:
• Create events, set up tickets and publish
• See registrations and attendees in real time
• Check people in by QR, one by one or a whole group, even without signal
• Run live polls, Q&A and quizzes, and put results on a projector
• Print badges and passes on A4 or label printers
• Answer attendee requests in the Assist inbox
• See page views, tracking links and where registrations come from
• Handle refunds and see your payouts
• Judge hackathons and competitions
• Scan leads at sponsor booths

Built in Hyderabad for clubs, colleges, communities and startups across India. Download Unifesto free.
```
