Brand guidelines · October 2026

01 · Who we are

Run the whole event.
Not just the ticket page.

Boilerplate (use as written)

Unifesto is an event platform for clubs, communities, institutions and startups in India. Organisers run ticketing, QR check-in, payouts, certificates, sponsors and hackathon judging from one Space. Attendees discover events, register and carry their pass in the Unifesto app.

Tagline

Optimize.
Organize.
Elevate.

Name

Always Unifesto: one word, capital U in text. Never UniFesto, UNIFESTO or Uni Festo. The lowercase form belongs to the logo only.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines02

02 · Logo

The wordmark

Silver on dark. The primary logo. Ink (#111113) on light.

Clear space. Keep empty space around the wordmark at least the height of its "u" on every side. Nothing else enters that zone.

Minimum size. 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that, use the symbol.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines03

03 · Symbol & icon

The u

App icon. Silver u, starlight and horizon on black.

Favicon and small sizes: tile and u only, no stars or horizon.

The symbol alone: avatars, stickers, merchandise, and anywhere the wordmark would fall below its minimum size.

The u is the first letter of the wordmark, unchanged. Use it when the brand is already named nearby (an app grid, a profile photo), and the full wordmark everywhere else.

07 · Powered by

For events run on Unifesto

Organisers can show the badge on event pages, posters, standees and sponsor walls. Use the boxed badge on photos and busy backgrounds, and the one-colour version on a plain colour.

Dark badgeLight badgeWhite, on dark coloursBlack, on light colours

Size. At least 120 px wide on screen and 30 mm in print. Stacked versions suit square spaces.

Place. Corners or the footer, with clear space of half the badge height. Never over faces or text.

Link. Online, link the badge to unifesto.app. The ready-made snippet is on unifesto.app/press.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines08

07 · Powered by in use

On organiser posters

On a photo: boxed dark badge in a corner.

On a plain colour: one-colour white, in the footer.

On a light colour: one-colour black, beside sponsors.

Square space: stacked badge, top corner.

The badge signs off the organiser's poster. Their colours lead; the badge stays small and in a corner or the footer.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines09

08 · Products

Stay, Huddle, Sprint and Orbit

Each product carries the wordmark with its name beside it. The lockup follows the logo's rules: silver on dark, ink on light, same clear space.

Unifesto StayUnifesto Huddle · live online sessionsUnifesto Sprint · hackathonsUnifesto Orbit · campus programme

Name. In text, always the full name: Unifesto Huddle, never Huddle alone on first mention.

Build. Use the supplied lockup. Don't type a product name next to the wordmark yourself.

Size. At least 160 px wide on screen, 40 mm in print, so the name stays readable.
