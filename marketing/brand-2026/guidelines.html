Unifesto Brand Guidelines · Horizon02

02 · The theme

Unifesto Horizon

Our visual theme on the website, Space, Apex and the app: night first, thin lines, and colour only where the work is. Locked on 29 October 2026. Changing a colour, line, light, glass effect or typeface needs a new edition of these guidelines.

Night first. Black ground and silver type. By day the same roles sit on paper #F2F2F5 with white sections and cards, and light turns pale blue.

Thin lines only. Stars, two white orbit hairlines, edge rules and the horizon, with a soft blue glow rising off it. Never planets, bars or coloured lines.

Colour lives in cards. Mint, sky and ivory sit inside cards, on the work they describe. Navigation, links and buttons stay monochrome.

Colour blends. Inside a card, an accent's soft shapes (orbs, a horizon, a beam, a fan) melt into a deep base. No lines, no hard edges.

Grain on everything. A fine film grain over every surface, heavier inside shade cards.

Frosted glass floats. Panels over a shade are frosted, untinted glass. Statuses are plain text, never pills.

03 · Logo

The wordmark

Silver on dark. The primary logo. Ink (#111113) on light.

Clear space. Keep empty space around the wordmark at least the height of its "u" on every side. Nothing else enters that zone.

Minimum size. 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that, use the symbol.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines · Horizon04

04 · Symbol & icon

The u

App icon. Silver u centred on black, with starlight and the Horizon-blue glow. By day, an ink u on paper; iOS follows the system appearance.

Favicon and small sizes: tile and u only, no stars or horizon.

The symbol alone: avatars, stickers, merchandise, and anywhere the wordmark would fall below its minimum size.

The u is the first letter of the wordmark, unchanged. Use it when the brand is already named nearby (an app grid, a profile photo), and the full wordmark everywhere else.

06 · Typography

Two typefaces

Headlines · DM Sans 500 (Google Fonts)

Aa Bb 123

One event, two sides of the door.

Headlines only. One weight, never bold or condensed. Tracking tightens as size grows: 0.9 px − 0.062 em.

Text · Hanken Grotesk (Google Fonts)

Aa Bb 123

Find events near you, register in a few taps, and walk in with the pass on your phone.

Body, UI and captions. Light 300 to Semibold 600.

The wordmark is drawn artwork, not typed text. Always use the supplied files.

Unifesto Brand Guidelines · Horizon08

07 · Lines & light

The sky behind every hero

Stars. Two layers of faint 1 px points, by night only.

Orbits. Two 1 px hairlines: a large circle from the top right, a wide ellipse rising from the bottom left. They drift too slowly to notice.

Horizon blue. A wide, flat glow rising off the horizon: from the bottom-right corner behind the hero's product, and along the foot of the page. #4D8DFF by night, a pale #8FB4FF by day. Never on the orbits.

Horizon. One line of light with a soft bloom, white by night and pale blue by day. It anchors heroes and closes pages.

Edge rules and grain. Faint vertical rules near each side; film grain at 12% by night, 9% by day.

08 · Shade cards

Product on a shade

Soft shapes in one accent, fully blended into a deep base: two orbs for sky, a horizon rising from below for mint, a diagonal beam for ivory, a fan of light for blue. Grain on top (128 px tile, overlay, 60%), and the product floats on it as frosted glass.

Each product carries the wordmark with its name beside it. The lockup follows the logo's rules: silver on dark, ink on light, same clear space.
