### Title

Unifesto Orbit: Lead your campus

(Alternative: Unifesto Orbit: every club on campus, in one orbit)

### Description

Every club on campus, in one orbit.

Unifesto Orbit is our campus programme for students. One Orbit Lead per campus represents Unifesto, not the college, and helps every club on campus run its events on Unifesto. Remote and flexible, with no fixed hours.

Apply in about ten minutes: https://unifesto.app/orbit/apply

The job, in four stops
1. Map: make a list of every club on your campus.
2. Onboard: each club creates its Space on Unifesto and joins your campus.
3. Activate: the club publishes its first event. That makes it an active club.
4. Support: be the first help for organisers on your campus.

Start with 30 days. Onboard 2 clubs in your first 30 days. After that there is no end date. You stay as long as you are active.

What you earn. Rewards are recognition, not cash.
Lead (pass the first 30 days): Orbit certificate, Orbit badge, the Lead welcome kit
Bronze (5 active clubs): letter of recommendation signed by the founder, a feature on Unifesto's socials
Silver (10 active clubs and 1 paid event): free Pro plan for your own club, a limited-edition hoodie every quarter
Gold (15 active clubs, or bring your fest onto Unifesto): fast-track interview for a paid internship, a shot at Orbit Lead of the Year

Who it is for: 18 or older, studying at the campus you would lead, active in a club or fest team. Big campuses also get 2 to 3 Orbit Crew who work with the Lead.

College not on Unifesto yet? Apply anyway. We create the campus Space.

How Orbit works: https://unifesto.app/orbit
Programme terms: https://unifesto.app/orbit/terms
Questions: orbit@unifesto.app

The job comes in four stops:
1. Map every club on campus
2. Onboard each club onto Unifesto
3. Activate: the club publishes its first event
4. Support organisers as the first point of help

It is remote and flexible, with no fixed hours. The first 30 days have one goal: onboard 2 clubs. After that there is no end date.

Rewards are recognition, not cash. A verifiable certificate, a badge that grows from Lead to Bronze, Silver and Gold, a welcome kit, goodies every quarter, a letter of recommendation, and for our Gold Leads a fast-track interview for a paid internship.

If you are a student who already runs things on campus, or you know one who does, this is for you.
