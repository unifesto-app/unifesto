Every campus runs on its clubs. The coding club, the dance crew, the E-Cell, the fest committee. Each one runs events, and each one figures out registrations, payments and check-ins on its own.

Unifesto Orbit changes that, one campus at a time. It is our new campus programme for students, and applications are open now.

**[Apply to be an Orbit Lead](https://unifesto.app/orbit/apply)** · [Watch the 90-second video](<YOUTUBE_URL>)

## What Orbit is

There is one Orbit Lead per campus. The Lead represents Unifesto, not their college, and helps every club on campus run its events on Unifesto.

It is remote and flexible, with no fixed hours. You work from your own campus, with the clubs you already know.

## The job, in four stops

1. **Map.** Make a list of every club on your campus.
2. **Onboard.** Each club creates its Space on Unifesto and joins your campus.
3. **Activate.** The club publishes its first event. That makes it an active club.
4. **Support.** Be the first help for organisers on your campus.

One more: introduce your fest committee to Unifesto.

## Start with 30 days

Your first 30 days are your start period. The goal is simple: onboard 2 clubs.

- Join the Orbit Leads WhatsApp community on day one
- Onboard 2 clubs in your first 30 days
- No end date after that. You stay as long as you are active.
- Exam breaks are fine. Tell your Program Lead before you go and pick up again after.

## What you earn

Rewards are recognition, not cash. An active club is one that has published its first event on Unifesto.

| Tier | What it takes | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | Pass the first 30 days | Orbit certificate, Orbit badge, the Lead welcome kit |
| Bronze | 5 active clubs | Letter of recommendation signed by the founder, a feature on Unifesto's socials |
| Silver | 10 active clubs and 1 paid event | Free Pro plan for your own club, a limited-edition hoodie every quarter |
| Gold | 15 active clubs, or bring your fest onto Unifesto | Fast-track interview for a paid internship, a shot at Orbit Lead of the Year, a limited-edition hoodie every quarter |

Your Orbit certificate is issued through Unifesto with a link anyone can verify. Your badge levels up with your tier.

### The welcome kit

Pass your first 30 days and a box lands at your door: the Orbit hoodie, the Orbit Playbook (pitches, scripts and your first 30 days in one book), a signed welcome letter from our founder, a sticker pack, and pins, a phone grip and a keychain.

### Goodies every quarter

Every active Lead gets a sticker and lanyard pack after each quarter closes. Silver and Gold Leads also get that quarter's limited-edition hoodie, a new design each time. Active means you have passed your first 30 days and added a club or event in the last 60 days.

## Orbit Crew

Big campuses get 2 to 3 Orbit Crew, chosen with the Lead. Crew help their Lead, with no targets or tiers of their own. They get the Crew welcome kit once their Lead passes the first 30 days, and an Orbit certificate.

## Who can apply

- You are 18 or older
- You are currently studying at the campus you would lead
- You are active in a club or fest team
- You are fine with remote work and no fixed hours

College not on Unifesto yet? Apply anyway. We create the campus Space.

## Ground rules

- Never say you represent your college. You represent Unifesto.
- Never collect money outside Unifesto.
- No fake clubs or events.
- No spam.
- When you post about Unifesto, say you are an Orbit Lead and tag #UnifestoOrbit.

## How to apply

The application takes about ten minutes. Pick your campus, tell us your year, the clubs you are part of and why you want the role, then verify your email and phone.

What happens next:

1. **We read it.** Our Program Lead reviews every application, usually within a week.
2. **A short call.** 15 minutes about your campus, its clubs and how you would start.
3. **You are appointed.** You get admin access to your campus Space on Unifesto.
4. **Accept the terms.** The Orbit Lead Programme Terms appear in the app. Accept them to begin.
5. **Your first 30 days.** Onboard 2 clubs to pass your start period.
