A team registration form for a college fest needs the team name, the event, the team lead's contact details, and each member's name, phone, email, college and year. Below is a copy-ready template, the five mistakes that break team forms, and a way to let each member fill in their own details instead.

It is written for organisers of hackathons, quizzes, robotics, dance and debate events, where people enter as a team of 2 to 6.

## The template

Copy these fields into any form tool. Mark the ones with a star as required.

### Team details (filled once, by the team lead)

| Field | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Team name * | Short text, 3 to 30 characters | Shown on the leaderboard and certificates |
| Event * | Dropdown | One form can cover several team events |
| Team size * | Number, with your minimum and maximum | Lets you check the member list |
| College * | Short text or dropdown | For prizes, travel and reporting |
| Team lead name * | Short text | Your one point of contact |
| Team lead phone * | Phone | For event-day calls |
| Team lead email * | Email | For confirmations and the pass |

### Member details (one block per member)

| Field | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Full name * | Short text | Exactly as it should appear on the certificate |
| Phone * | Phone | One per member, so you can reach anyone |
| Email * | Email | Each member gets their own confirmation |
| Year of study | Dropdown | Some events are first-years only |
| Roll number or college ID | Short text | Only if the gate checks IDs |
| T-shirt size | Dropdown | Only if you give kits |

### Event-specific (add only what you will use)

| Event | Extra field |
|---|---|
| Hackathon | GitHub or portfolio link, problem statement choice |
| Quiz | None |
| Robotics | Robot weight class, a short description |
| Dance or drama | Song or script, run time |
| Paper presentation | Abstract upload (PDF) |

## Five mistakes that break team forms

1. **One response for the whole team.** The lead types four people's details into one box, and half of them are wrong.
2. **Payment outside the form.** A UPI ID in the description means matching screenshots to teams by hand.
3. **No size check.** Teams of 7 register for an event capped at 4, and you find out at the gate.
4. **Members can't be changed.** Someone drops out and the lead fills the whole form again, so you get duplicates.
5. **One pass for the team.** If only the lead has the confirmation, the rest of the team waits outside.

## The better way: a group ticket

A group ticket turns the team form around. The lead registers and pays once, then each member claims a seat with their own account and fills in their own details.

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team lead | Picks the group ticket, answers the team questions, pays once |
| 2 | Team lead | Shares the invite link with the team |
| 3 | Each member | Opens the link, claims a seat, answers the member questions |
| 4 | Each member | Gets their own QR pass |
| 5 | Organiser | Sees each team with its seats and who has claimed one, and can remove a member or change the lead |

This fixes the five mistakes: every member types their own details, payment happens once at registration, the team size is set on the ticket, a seat can change hands without a new registration, and everyone has a pass.

## FAQ

### What should a team registration form include?

A team registration form should include the team name, the event, the team size, the team lead's name, phone and email, and each member's name, phone, email and year. Add event-specific fields only if you will use them.

### How do I make a team registration form in Google Forms?

Add a team details section, then one section per member with name, phone and email. Use "Go to section based on answer" on team size so teams only see as many member sections as they have.

### How do I collect payment for team registration?

Use a group ticket that the team lead pays for once at registration. This avoids matching UPI screenshots to teams after the form closes.

### Can each team member register separately?

Yes, with a group ticket. The lead pays and shares an invite link, and each member claims their own seat and fills in their own details.

### What if a team member drops out?

On Unifesto an unclaimed seat can go to someone else, and the organiser can remove a member or make another member the lead from the team's seats, so the team does not register again. In a Google Form you usually delete the response and ask for a new one.
