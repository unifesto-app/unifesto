## Before you record

- Same setup as W1: space.unifesto.app, Space **Aurora Tech Club**, 1440x900, zoom 110%, crop or blur the phone number in the header.
- **Use Aurora Fest 2026** (ticket types, Squad of 4) and **Build Night** (Team of 3). Both are seeded demo events.
- **Never open the attendee list or group seats on camera without blurring.** They show `demo.N@example.com` and the seats screen shows `demo_pay_` IDs. Safer: show the Squad of 4 ticket detail (seats, check-in, revenue) instead of the attendee rows.
- Don't save new tickets, questions or codes on the demo. Fill the form, show it, Cancel.
- The buyer side happens in the Unifesto app. **Don't film the app's 5.0 screens** (teaser-only until 2 Dec). If you show buying, use a short screen recording of the current store app (4.x), or skip it and say "attendees pay in the Unifesto app".
- Fee line: say only what is settled (see blocker 1 in `README.md`). The script below avoids a percentage on purpose.
