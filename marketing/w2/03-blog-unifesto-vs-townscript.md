Unifesto is a Townscript alternative built for college clubs, fests and communities in India that run many events with one team. Townscript is a general ticketing platform that is better if you sell in foreign currencies or want a ticket widget on your own website.

This comparison is as of 30 Oct 2026. Prices come from each company's public pricing page.

## Quick answer

| You need | Better choice |
|---|---|
| Sell tickets in currencies other than INR | Townscript |
| A ticket widget on your own website or Facebook page | Townscript |
| Attendees who can register in a browser, with no app | Townscript |
| One home for a club's team, events and history | Unifesto |
| Roles per event for curators and crew | Unifesto |
| Group tickets where each member claims their own seat | Unifesto |
| Hackathons with teams, rounds and judging | Unifesto (Sprint, Pro plan) |
| Free RSVP events with no fee | Both |

## Where Townscript is better

- **More currencies.** Townscript sells tickets in many currencies. Unifesto takes Indian rupees only.
- **A lower listed fee on Starter.** Townscript lists a 2% platform fee against 2.5% on Unifesto Starter.
- **No app for attendees.** Townscript attendees buy on the web. Unifesto attendees register in the Unifesto app.
- **Sell from your own site.** Townscript offers ticket sales from your website and Facebook page. Unifesto has no embeddable widget.
- **Start without approval.** Townscript lets you sign up, link a bank account and start selling. A Unifesto Space is reviewed by our team before its first event goes live.
- **Years of track record.** Townscript says over 300,000 organisers have used it. Unifesto has 70 Spaces live and 10,000+ attendees, as of October 2026.

## Where Unifesto is better

- **Built for teams that run many events.** A club's Space holds every event, member and report, and moves to next year's team.
- **Access that matches the job.** A Curator runs an event, a Co-Curator helps, Crew scan at the gate. Space Admins decide what each role can do.
- **Teams register properly.** The team lead pays once, and each member claims a seat, answers their own questions and gets their own pass.
- **Hackathons in the same place.** Unifesto Sprint runs teams, problem statements, rounds, judging and a help desk on Pro.
- **After the event.** Certificates with public verification, PDF reports and a money view per event.

## What Unifesto does not do (yet)

- Unifesto takes payment in Indian rupees only.
- Attendees register in the Unifesto app, not on the web.
- Unifesto has no ticket widget for your own website.
- Unifesto does not run live polls or Q&A during an event yet.
- Unifesto does not bring you an audience by itself.

## Switching from Townscript

1. Request a Space for your club or community.
2. Add your team and set their roles.
3. Create the event, its ticket types and questions.
4. Import past attendees from a CSV into the event's attendee list if you need them.
5. Share the event link.

## FAQ

### What are the best Townscript alternatives in India?

For college clubs, fests and hackathons, Unifesto is a Townscript alternative built around a team Space, roles per event, group tickets and Unifesto Sprint for hackathons. Other options include KonfHub, Devfolio for hackathons, Eventbrite and Luma.

### How much does Townscript charge?

As of 30 Oct 2026, Townscript's pricing page lists a 2% Townscript fee plus ₹0 per ticket and a 2% payment gateway fee for domestic payments in India, exclusive of taxes. Free events are not charged.

### How much does Unifesto charge compared to Townscript?

As of 30 Oct 2026, Unifesto Starter is ₹0 a month with a 2.5% platform fee on paid tickets, and Pro is ₹1,499 a month with a 1.5% fee, at launch pricing. Razorpay's gateway charges are separate, and prices exclude 18% GST.

### Does Unifesto support international currencies like Townscript?

No. Unifesto takes payment in Indian rupees only, as of 30 Oct 2026. Townscript sells tickets in many currencies.

### Is Unifesto better than Townscript for college fests?

Unifesto is built for college clubs and fests that run many events with one team, with roles per event and group tickets. Townscript is better if you need other currencies, a website widget or web-only registration.

### Can I move my events from Townscript to Unifesto?

Yes. Create the event in your Space, and import past attendees from a CSV into the attendee list if you need them.
