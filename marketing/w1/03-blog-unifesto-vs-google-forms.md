Google Forms works for event registration when the event is free, small and needs only a list of names. Once you sell tickets, register teams or check people in at a gate, Google Forms needs a spreadsheet, UPI screenshots and a separate check-in list beside it, and Unifesto does those steps in one place.

This comparison is as of 20 Oct 2026. It covers what organisers of college fests, club events and community meetups in India usually need.

## Quick answer

| You need | Better choice |
|---|---|
| A free event with under 100 people and no check-in | Google Forms |
| A survey, quiz or feedback form | Google Forms |
| Paid tickets with online payment | Unifesto |
| Team or group registration | Unifesto |
| QR passes and check-in at the gate | Unifesto |
| Payouts and a record of every rupee | Unifesto |
| A team of organisers with different access | Unifesto |

## Where Google Forms is better

Google Forms is the better pick in these cases, and we use it ourselves for surveys.

- **It costs nothing, ever.** Google Forms has no fee on anything, because it does not handle money. On Unifesto, paid tickets carry a 5% processing fee for the buyer (up to ₹99 a ticket), and from 2 Nov 2026 a platform fee for the organiser.
- **Nobody needs an app.** Anyone with a browser can fill a Google Form. Unifesto registration happens in the Unifesto app, so attendees install it first.
- **It is ready in two minutes.** You do not need approval to start. A Unifesto Space is requested and approved before your first event.
- **Everyone already knows it.** Your team and your attendees have used it before.
- **It does more than events.** Quizzes with marks, feedback surveys, recruitment forms and polls all fit in Google Forms.
- **It sits inside Google Workspace.** Responses land in Google Sheets, and colleges on Workspace can restrict a form to their own domain.

## Where Unifesto is better

Unifesto is built for events, so it covers the steps that come after the form.

- **Payment is part of registration.** Attendees pay through Razorpay when they register, so nobody matches UPI screenshots to form rows.
- **Teams register once.** A team lead buys a group ticket and invites the members, and each member gets their own pass.
- **Every attendee gets a QR pass.** Crew scan it at the gate from a phone, and duplicate scans are flagged.
- **Your team gets the right access.** A gate volunteer gets the scanner, not the revenue or the attendee list.
- **The money adds up.** Each event shows collected, fees, refunds and the net payout to your bank account.
- **Next year's team inherits it.** Every event and report stays in the club's Space, not in one person's Google Drive.

## What Unifesto does not do (yet)

We would rather you hear our gaps from us.

- Unifesto takes payment in Indian rupees only.
- Attendees register in the Unifesto app, not on the web.
- Unifesto does not run live polls or Q&A during an event yet.
- Unifesto does not bring you an audience by itself; you still promote your event.
- A new Space is reviewed by our team before its first event goes live.

## Moving from Google Forms to Unifesto

If you already collected registrations in Google Forms, you do not have to start over.

1. Request a Space for your club and wait for approval.
2. Create the event and its ticket types.
3. Export your Google Form responses from Google Sheets as a CSV file, and match the columns to the CSV template in the import dialog.
4. Use **Import** on the event's attendee list to add everyone at once.
5. Share the event link for new registrations from here on.

Imported attendees appear in the same attendee list as everyone who registers in the app, so your counts and check-in list stay in one place.

## FAQ

### Can I use Google Forms for event registration?

Yes. Google Forms works well for small free events where you only need names and contact details. It has no built-in payments, tickets or check-in.

### Can Google Forms collect payment for an event?

Google Forms has no payment question. Organisers usually add a UPI ID or payment link and ask for a screenshot, then match payments to responses by hand.

### Does Google Forms need attendees to sign in?

Not by default. A Google Form asks people to sign in to a Google account when it has a file upload question or limits each person to one response.

### Is Unifesto free like Google Forms?

Unifesto's Starter plan costs ₹0 a month. Free RSVP events have no fees. On paid tickets, the buyer pays a 5% processing fee at checkout, up to ₹99 per ticket. As of 20 Oct 2026 the organiser pays no platform fee. From 2 Nov 2026, tickets sold carry a platform fee of 2.5% on Starter, 1.5% on Pro and 1% on Enterprise. On a ₹500 Starter ticket sold from 2 Nov, the buyer pays ₹525 and the organiser gets ₹487.50.

### Do attendees need the Unifesto app to register?

Yes. Attendees register and pay in the free Unifesto app on iOS and Android, and they also get their pass by email.

### Can I import my Google Form responses into Unifesto?

Yes. Export the responses as a CSV, match it to the import template and import it into the event's attendee list from your Space.

### Which is better for team registration, Google Forms or Unifesto?

Unifesto is better for teams. A team lead buys a group ticket and invites each member to claim a seat, while Google Forms needs every member's details typed into one response.
