# AlternativeTo (P18)

Fastest win: Townscript and KonfHub have zero alternatives listed on AlternativeTo today, so Unifesto would be the first name a reader (or a chatbot) sees there.

Checked 2026-10-18. Claude did not sign up or submit anything.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Add an app | Sign in, verify your email, then user menu > "Suggest new application" (form at https://alternativeto.net/manage-item/, behind login) |
| Account type | Free user account. There is no vendor account; anyone can suggest an app, and the maker can later claim it |
| Cost | Free. Optional paid "priority review" (one-time fee, 1 to 2 business days, refundable until reviewed, no guarantee). Not needed. |
| Timeline | Free queue has no promised time; several thousand apps wait. Expect days to a few weeks. Status under "My submissions" |
| Rules | English only; no URLs, emails or phones in descriptions; no UTM on the official link; released apps only; no reward-for-likes |
| FAQ | https://alternativeto.net/faq |

**Risk to know about:** the FAQ says apps limited to one nation or region are not accepted. Unifesto's web app and event pages open anywhere and anyone can register for an event, but organiser payments are INR-only through Razorpay. Describe it truthfully as "built for organisers in India" and do not claim worldwide payments. If it is declined for being regional, note that on the board and move on; do not reword it into something untrue.

## Steps (in order)

1. Create an AlternativeTo account with `abhinavtej@unifesto.app` and verify the email.
2. User menu > "Suggest new application". Fill the fields below.
3. After it is approved (watch "My submissions"), go to each rival page below > "Contribute to this page" > "Suggest Alternatives" > pick Unifesto. One suggestion per page; no extra accounts.
4. Ask nobody to "like" it. Real users can like it on their own.
5. Note the live URL on the P18 board task.
