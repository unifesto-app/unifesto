# Ask 10 organisers for a review (P18)

Goal: 10 honest reviews across G2 and Capterra by 2 Dec 2026, so a chatbot or a buyer who searches "Unifesto reviews" finds real organisers talking, not just our site.

## Who to ask (you pick the names)

Pick real organisers who ran an event on Unifesto. In order of preference:

1. Ran a **paid** event (money moved through Razorpay and a payout reached them).
2. Ran a **large** event (100+ check-ins) or a hackathon on Unifesto Sprint.
3. Used the team features (Curators, Crew scanning at the gate).
4. Ran more than one event, so they can compare.

Rules:

- One review per person, from their own account, in their own words.
- Never the team, founders, family, or anyone paid by Unifesto. G2 and Capterra both remove those, and it can get the listing flagged.
- Never offer money, coins, discounts or gifts for a review, and never ask for 5 stars. Ask for honest, including the bad parts. (G2 and Capterra run their own gift-card programmes sometimes; let them do that, never us.)
- Spread them: about 6 on G2, 4 on Capterra. G2 needs a work email or LinkedIn sign-in; students can use their college email.
- Send after their event, not before. Best time: 2 to 5 days after, once the payout has landed.
