## Steps (in order)

1. Open https://www.saasworthy.com/offerings and click "Get Free Listing".
2. Fill the form with the values below and submit.
3. When their team emails, reply with the facts below and attach the logo and screenshots.
4. Once live, sign in to the vendor portal, check the profile against `facts.md`, and fix anything wrong (especially pricing and features).
5. Note the URL on the P18 board task.
