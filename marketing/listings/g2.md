## Profile (after claiming, my.G2 > Product Profile)

**What is Unifesto? (overview, up to 500 words)**

> Unifesto is an event platform for clubs, communities, institutions and startups in India. Organisers run ticketing, QR check-in, payouts, certificates, sponsors and hackathon judging from one Space. Attendees discover events, register and carry their pass in the Unifesto app on iOS and Android.
>
> A Space holds the whole organising team. Space Admins add Curators, Co-Curators and Crew, each with their own permissions, and can tune the permission matrix. Organisers sell free, paid and group tickets through Razorpay, ask custom registration questions with file uploads and show-if logic, and run coupons, waitlists and sub-events. Crew scan QR passes from the app at several check-in points, such as the gate, lunch and kit collection; duplicate scans are flagged and groups check in by roster.
>
> Money is visible end to end. Attendees pay through Razorpay, organisers request payouts to their bank account, refunds go through a two-step approval, and the financials view shows collected amounts, fees, refunds and net payout for every event.
>
> Unifesto Sprint runs hackathons: teams, problem statements, rounds with weighted rubrics, judge panels, rooms with volunteers, a help desk, submissions, a live leaderboard and results. Unifesto Stay handles event accommodation. Certificates come with a template designer and public verification links, and sponsors get a portal with booth lead capture.
>
> Free RSVP events cost nothing. Paid tickets carry a 2.5% platform fee on the free Starter plan at launch pricing. Unifesto is built in Hyderabad and currently supports INR payments only.

## Review rules (G2)

- Never pay, gift or discount for a review on your own. If we ever incentivise, it must be disclosed to G2 and must not depend on the rating.
- Do not cherry-pick only happy organisers. Ask everyone on the `review-ask.md` list, whatever their experience.
- Reviewers are verified (often via LinkedIn), so team members and friends will be rejected and can get the profile flagged.
